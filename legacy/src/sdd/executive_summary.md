## Architecture at a Glance
Details: [c4-context.md](c4-context.md) / [architecture.md](architecture.md)

IAS is designed as a **Modular Monolith** using **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**. It features three strictly isolated Bounded Contexts:
1. **Media Ingestion**: Handles input from various sources (YouTube, local files).
2. **Speech Processing**: Manages the conversion of audio to text using AI models.
3. **Knowledge Compilation**: Structures raw text into usable knowledge units.

The system follows **Hexagonal Architecture** principles, ensuring that the business logic (Domain/Application) is entirely independent of infrastructure concerns (PostgreSQL, Redis, OpenAI/Local AI).
